Support

How can we help?

For account, billing, privacy, or technical issues, email intusketch@gmail.com. Please include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened.

Subscriptions

Purchases run through Apple. Manage or cancel under Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Refunds go through Apple Support.

Portrait generation

Generation can take time and may fail with weak networks or unusable photos. Try a clear face photo, stay online, and write us if a job stays stuck.

Privacy & deletion

Request account deletion in the app where available—or by email. Privacy questions can go to the same address.

Friend feedback links

Share links only with people you trust. If a link slipped out by mistake, email us with details so we can help.
