The blind spot is the gap between how you see yourself and how others read you.

IntuSketch does not pick a winner. It gives the gap a shape you can look at—shoulder to shoulder, without turning it into an argument.

How it works

Four steps—one moment that stays with you.

Upload a photo, choose what feels true, and add an outside perspective—it's in the comparison that you notice what you usually miss yourself. Presets, free writing, and structured traits anchor the portrait in what's observable, not subjective distortion.

  1. 01

    Describe your current state

    You pick simple presets and wording that match how you see yourself right now. That pins down the inputs before your image is processed.

  2. 02

    Upload a photo and generate your portrait

    You upload a photo. The system generates a portrait using the traits you chose in step one.

  3. 03

    Add an outside perspective

    You can send a private link to friends. They answer anonymously and honestly about how they see you from the outside. Their replies are folded in as extra inputs.

  4. 04

    Compare the results

    The app creates portraits:
    — self-perception
    — how your friends see you from the outside

    You see them side by side and compare directly.
    This helps surface blind spots in self-perception.

The reveal

The moment the second portrait appears—and the note on how you're seen from outside

It is not better or worse than the first.

It simply reflects another angle of perception.

IntuSketch helps you hold that difference and see the result not as a verdict, but as two views of one person—and as blind spots showing through.
